Cornerman Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains what the Cornerman iOS app (“Cornerman”, “we”) does with your information. In short: your training data stays on your iPhone, and the camera is never recorded.

Camera

The AI Coach uses your iPhone’s front camera to track your body position while you train. Every video frame is analyzed on your device and discarded right away. We never record, store or upload video, images or body-pose data, and we can’t see them.

Your profile and progress

Your answers from setup (styles, experience, goals, stance, schedule, an optional name) and your training history (lessons, XP, streaks, badges, AI Coach session stats) are stored only on your device. If your device backups are turned on, they are included in your iCloud or computer backup, which Apple manages. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it at any time in Profile › Settings › Reset all progress.

Notifications

If you turn on training reminders, Cornerman schedules local notifications on your device. No server is involved.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple shares only the information needed to confirm your subscription with the app.

No tracking, no analytics, no third parties

Cornerman contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking software, and doesn’t share any data with third parties. It doesn’t track you across apps or websites.

Children

Cornerman isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
