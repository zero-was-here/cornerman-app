Quick answers

  • The camera doesn’t see me. Prop your iPhone up about 2–3 m away with good light, so your whole body (feet included for stance drills) is in frame.
  • Manage or cancel my subscription. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions › Cornerman. Subscriptions are managed by Apple.
  • Restore my purchase. On the subscription screen, tap Restore Purchases.
  • Reset my progress. In the app: Profile › Settings › Reset all progress.
  • Is my video recorded? No. Video is analyzed on your device and never stored or uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.
